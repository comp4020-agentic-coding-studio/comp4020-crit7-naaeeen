# Process overview

Draft prepared with Codex from the project record, for my review before submission.

I asked Codex to investigate the existing ANU booking interface before implementing a replacement, and to use my Assignment 2 planning documents as a standard for research and verification. The first useful correction came from that investigation: the existing accessible interface already had equipment filters. Its “1–4 people” option nevertheless returned spaces for one, two and four people together. I chose to address complete-session matching, comparison across libraries, and clearer facilities and booking status. The research and scope are recorded in [5d33253](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-crit7-naaeeen/commit/5d33253).

The resulting prototype, Common Room, checks the whole requested interval and saves reservations in SQLite. Booking, cancellation, the daily allowance and duplicate submissions are enforced on the server. The data is clearly labelled as illustrative, and nothing connects to real ANU reservations. The implementation and its tests are in [d1765ff](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-crit7-naaeeen/commit/d1765ff).

I also asked for independent review, actual browser use and more purposeful motion. Those checks found problems that a successful build did not establish: invalid dates could interrupt error recovery, old errors could remain after a corrected search, and disconnected tabs could miss availability changes. The agent repaired these and tested the affected paths. The detailed record in [docs/PROCESS-EVIDENCE.md](docs/PROCESS-EVIDENCE.md) separates automated checks, observed browser behavior and remaining review work. The final judgement is whether someone can complete a study-space booking clearly on both a phone and a desktop.
