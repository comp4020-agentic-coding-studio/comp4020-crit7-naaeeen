# Crit 7 reflection

Draft prepared with Codex from my directions and the observed project record, for my review.

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was changing the question from finding a room to finding a complete study session. I asked for the existing system to be inspected before we assumed what it lacked. That mattered: its accessible interface already provided equipment filters, while its broad capacity categories still left a four-person group comparing spaces that only seated one or two people. I wanted the replacement to improve time matching, comparison between libraries, and the clarity of facilities and booking status together.

That gave the database and interface one shared promise: a result must fit the group for the entire requested period, and a confirmed reservation must survive a reload. It also made the conflict and cancellation tests relevant to an actual user journey.

## What did this change about who I want to be as a software developer?

The direction I set for this work was to research, plan and verify rather than assume that a convincing result was correct. I want to make that standard more concrete: explain the promise, identify what could break it, and check the running experience. The browser checks found focus and narrow-screen problems after the ordinary suite was green. For me, directing an agent well includes making room for that evidence to change the implementation. Visual polish and motion matter when they help a person understand and finish the task.
